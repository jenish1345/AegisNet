PIPELINE METHODOLOGY

The 7-Step Evidence Verification Pipeline

STEP 01

Message Ingestion

Normalizes incoming email, SMS, or chat text into standardized schema.

STEP 02

Signal Extraction

Identifies urgency language, domain spoofing, lure mechanics, and payment vectors.

STEP 03

Evidence Correlation

TraceX queries historical telemetry graph to correlate cross-channel indicators.

STEP 04

Campaign Reconstruction

Clusters evidence to expose overarching criminal threat networks and tactics.

STEP 05

Counter-Evidence Check

Actively evaluates benign markers, SPF/DKIM verification, and false-positive signs.

STEP 06

Claim Verification

Formally verifies specific assertions against known ground-truth databases.

STEP 07

Human Review Brief

Synthesizes executive summaries, limitations, and actionable recommended next steps.

🔗 TraceX Correlation Engine

TraceX links scam signals across time, channels, and domains. By analyzing counter-evidence alongside threat indicators, TraceX eliminates false positives and ensures analysts get verifiable evidence briefs.

Graph Relationship Intelligence

Maps connections between phone numbers, domains, crypto wallets, and phrasing patterns.

Balanced Counter-Evidence

Prevents false positives by explicitly searching for contradicting evidence before reaching conclusions.
